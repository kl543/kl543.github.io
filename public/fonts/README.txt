Academic Serif is a Latin subset of Source Serif 4, restricted to weights 400–600.
It was renamed because Adobe reserves the name Source under the SIL OFL.
Copyright and license notices are preserved in OFL.txt and the font metadata.
Upstream: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif
Original Latin WOFF2: Google Fonts, Source Serif 4 v15.
Only 400 and 600 are used by the website; the font is served locally.
